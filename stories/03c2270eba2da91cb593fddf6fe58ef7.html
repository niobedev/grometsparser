Part 7: Poltergeist

On Monday morning, Sonia found Carla perched on the break-room table, talking with both hands. Nate leaned against the counter beside the coffee machine. It was too early for either of them to look this awake.

“Sonia!” Carla called out, her voice carrying. “You’re not gonna believe this.”

Sonia forced a smile and approached, setting her bag down casually on a nearby chair. “Morning to you too,” she said lightly. “What’s got you both so worked up?”

“Artifacts,” Nate said, trying to sound calm. “Some of them activated yesterday.”

Sonia turned to the coffee machine before answering.

“Activated how?” She kept her eyes on the cup.

“You know the black gunk we’ve been taking off?” Carla said. “Well, underneath it the things light up. And hum. Yesterday, late morning. I was about ready to fetch salt and start chanting.”

“The cleaned ones,” Nate said. “The pieces we’d exposed to sunlight. Around eleven yesterday there were vibrations and flickers of light.” He glanced at Carla. “No chanting.”

Eleven. Sonia held the mug with both hands. She had been fastening the collar round Marion’s neck then. She could give them the missing part of their story, if she were willing to explain what she had taken home.

“What kind of activity?” she asked, forcing a casual tone. “And which artifacts are we talking about?”

“Oh, you’ll love this,” Carla said, her grin turning mischievous. “Artifact sixty-nine—yeah, I know, nice number for a C-belt—and artifact three-seventy-two. The cyber-chastity belt and the punishment heels.”

“Could we use the catalog names?” Nate asked.

“I am using the catalog numbers. You’ve seen the objects.”

Sonia frowned, her curiosity piqued despite herself. “Cyber-chastity belt? Punishment heels?”

Carla smirked, gesturing as she spoke. “Okay, so artifact sixty-nine is this set of metal panties—lockable, rigid, but with these weird internal mechanisms. Officially, its purpose is unknown. Unofficially, have a look at the shape. And artifact three-seventy-two is a pair of ankle-breaking heels, like, sky-high stilettos with lockable ankle straps. I tried them on once just for fun, and they’re impossible to walk in.”

Nate straightened away from the counter. “You tried them on? Carla, do you want us fired?”

“Oh, relax,” Carla said, waving him off. “It was weeks ago, and nothing happened. Anyway, the belt and the heels both started doing their thing yesterday. The belt was humming—like, actually vibrating—and the heels? They lit up. Little blue lines along the straps and the soles. I half-expected them to start walking on their own.”

Sonia tried to find another explanation for the timing. She could not. Somehow the collar at home belonged to the same system as these things.

“And this all started at the same time?” Sonia asked, trying to sound calm.

“Within a few minutes,” Nate said. “Five thousand years, Sonia. And we’re standing here discussing which ones work.”

“Yeah,” Carla added, grinning. “Forget pyramids and aqueducts. These guys were building high-tech chastity belts and designer torture heels. Priorities, right?”

Sonia gave the laugh Carla expected. She had come in hoping to get through an ordinary Monday; now she was listening for anything she could use without explaining why she needed it.

At her desk, Sonia read the artifact catalog with growing unease. The collar was one of many devices built to control people.

She scrolled through the images. Wrist shackles lay under millennia of grime, their seams suggesting locks inside. Anklet cuffs, their edges lined with groves that might become glowing lines, as they did on the collar. Elbow shackles designed to bind the arms tightly behind the back. Thigh bands that paired seamlessly with chastity belts, forcing the knees chastely together.

There were chastity bras with curved, rigid cups and wide, locking straps, corsets that seemed more like full body chastity cages, and hoods with smooth, featureless faces. Metal blindfolds etched with patterns that shimmered faintly when exposed to light. Then there were the inserts—Sonia’s stomach churned as she flipped past them. They came in every imaginable shape and size, and for all orifices.

The team had cataloged, photographed, and stored hundreds of artifacts. Whoever these people had been, control and restraint weren’t just a part of their lives—they had been an obsession. Their technology restrained the body and seemed to control the mind.

Sonia rubbed her temples. She couldn’t stop thinking about the collar, about the way Marion had looked at her, the way the hum had resonated through her body like it belonged there. Last night she had dreamed of that power.

She could check the pieces out for restoration. There would be a form, a name beside each number. Her name.

Sonia moved the cursor back to the catalog records. For a while she did nothing. Then she began deleting.

It took her only a few minutes to erase the records for a handful of artifacts: a chastity belt, its matching thigh bands, and a set of shackles for the wrists and elbows. They weren’t the best-preserved examples in the collection, but they were intact enough to serve her purposes—whatever those might turn out to be.

Sonia entered the quiet, cool storage room. Labeled artifacts filled the shelves. She moved quickly, scanning the labels for the items she’d flagged.

Her heart raced as she found them one by one. The belt was heavier than she expected, its metal cool against her hands. The shackles were smooth and unyielding, looking ready to use. She bundled the items carefully, her movements quick and precise.

At the back of the storage room, near the janitor’s supplies, she opened the door to a dusty broom closet. The shelves inside were cluttered with old equipment and forgotten tools—perfect for hiding something she wasn’t ready to claim. Sonia placed the artifacts on the lowest shelf, pushing them as far back as she could.

She closed the closet and left her hand on the handle. The pieces were still in the building. She could put them back. She had been careful enough with the records that she might even call it a clerical error.

All that care was rather difficult to explain to herself.

Walking away, she told herself she could still stop. But she kept thinking about Marion in the collar.

Back at her desk, Sonia stared at the screen. She could almost hear the collar, though it was at home. Her hands hovered over the keyboard.

Her thoughts drifted, unbidden, to Marion. Marion kneeling before her, the collar gleaming around her neck, her eyes wide with submission and longing. But it wasn’t just the collar in this fantasy. The chastity belt was locked tightly around her hips, its unforgiving metal ensuring that no matter how much Marion squirmed, she could find no release. The thigh bands kept her knees together, the wrist and elbow shackles locking her arms securely behind her back, forcing her into a posture of complete helplessness.

Sonia’s breath hitched. She could see it so clearly: Marion looking up at her, her lips trembling with both hope and fear. Hope for release, for the touch that would grant her freedom—but also hope to be denied, to feel Sonia’s power hold her there, on the edge, tormented and utterly devoted.

She wanted complete control of Marion, beyond what any restraint could give her. The thought excited her more than she wanted to admit.

She closed her eyes, but still pictured Marion looking up at her. She could almost feel the weight of the ring in her hand again, the cool metal that had bridged the connection between them.

This is wrong, she told herself. This isn’t us.

Sonia forced herself to breathe deeply, muttering under her breath, “To control others, you must first learn to control yourself.”

She held the edge of the desk until she could feel the grain under her fingers. Down the hall, someone was typing. The air conditioner ran on. She counted her breaths and tried to attend to those ordinary sounds.

The fantasies weren’t new. Sonia had always harbored a quiet fascination with restraint and power dynamics. But this—this was different. It wasn’t playful or consensual, not the way it had been before. The collar had twisted it into something darker, more insidious. She did not want a shared experience. She wanted to own Marion without asking permission.

She opened her eyes. The page on the screen was still blank. For all the effort of sitting here, she had done no work.

As the day wore on, she kept returning to the collar and what she could do with it.
