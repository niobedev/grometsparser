“Still in her lab, trying to find a flaw in one of her inventions,” Summer explained, and her face lit up as the front door opened and the four missing women walked in. The usual greetings among friends followed, while Cass remained seated on the sofa. She didn’t know the others very well, and her body was grateful that she didn’t have to move for once. Instead, she stared at the screen with glazed eyes until the woman named Phaelyn stepped between them. Cass looked up at the shy girl who was holding out her hand.

“He… hey… my name is Phaelyn,” she said, stuttering, and smiled sheepishly.

“Cass,” Cass said curtly, but made no move to shake her hand. Somewhat disappointed, Phaelyn let hers drop. Ifry noticed it immediately and raised an eyebrow, but said nothing. She wanted to see how her friend handled the situation and learned from it.

“Sooo, you were at the last event too, right? How did you like it?” Phaelyn asked, and Cass took a deep breath. She’d really just wanted to watch a movie, not make new acquaintances.
