Once a little-used watering spot for desert nomads, it was now a lush, sophisticated establishment the girl had visited many times over the past few years. It was a vast country as well, and sparsely populated — at least here in the hinterland, deep into the hinterland, for the coast was a long way away. Strange that they both showed up so clearly when you’d have thought the all-pervading desert dust would have covered them.
