*Don’t move, don’t move*, I kept repeating, over and over. If that thrice-damned timer reset again I was sure to crack under the strain.

#### _**All Over***_

_I heard the door open. I cringed, waiting for that soul destroying beep._

_Nothing happened. I had been so obsessed with the timer it hadn’t dawned on me the door had swung open. That could only be Sally._

_“Only_two* resets, that’s a *remarkable* feat given the difficulties you must be *suffering*. Sally is *pleased* to see you passed your *lesson* in patience.”

I heard her kneel down next to me. Suddenly there was a sharp jerk on the Rod of Discipline. The pain was agonizing, but I held it in. She wasn’t going to trick me like that. If I broke my silence she’d have every justification to turn on that timer again. I wasn’t going to let her win this one.

“*Nothing* to say? Sally is so *glad* she has been able to *help* you. Once you set your *mind* to a *task*, and you have the *incentives* to keep at it, doesn’t it become *easier* over time? Sally thinks that’s *true.”*

The trash bag came off my head. I felt her loosening the straps binding my hands. First the outer, and then the inner cover came off. I didn’t immediately grab for the key. This might be some trick on her part. Sally was still very much the one with all the power.

“You may start *releasing* yourself. Sally has *fulfilled* her part in being your *backup*. Have a *good* night.” The door closed behind her.

I wasted no time in getting out of the restraints. When I was able to pull off the hood I was surprised to see the timer at my feet. Did she leave it for me deliberately? Temptation, get thee behind me.

By the time I finished Sally was long gone. This had been memorable. She had done it to me again. Curse her and her Jedi mind tricks. I had made it through and was able to walk away from yet another potential disaster. Could I ever go it solo again, without her *help*, as she called it?
